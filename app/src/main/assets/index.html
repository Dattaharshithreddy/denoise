Saves a .zip holding a lossless 24-bit WAV at the original sample rate.

How it works: the audio is split into overlapping 43 ms frames. A noise profile is measured, then each frequency bin gets a gain from a log-spectral amplitude estimator with speech-presence detection, which keeps voice detail while avoiding the warbling “musical noise” of simple gates. Hum is cut with narrow notch filters, and stereo images are kept intact. Works best on speech. Music with sustained notes can lose some tone at high settings.
